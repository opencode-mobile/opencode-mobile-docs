RemoteAuto Privacy Policy

How RemoteAuto handles data. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

RemoteAuto is an independent Android app. It works with Google Maps links, uses the Google Places API, and opens Waze. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Google or Waze. This page covers the RemoteAuto app only.

Who we are

RemoteAuto is an independent app from the publisher of OpenCode Mobile — Unofficial. It has no account system. We run no server for it, and nothing it handles is sent to us.

Shared links

You add a place by sharing it from Google Maps, or another app, to RemoteAuto through Android's share sheet. The app reads the text that is shared and looks for a Google Maps link in it.

Google Maps often shares a short link such as maps.app.goo.gl/…. To read it, RemoteAuto opens that link the way a browser would and follows it to the full Google Maps address, sometimes by loading the page and reading the address from it. These requests go to Google. They carry the link, your IP address, and a generic browser identifier. They carry no account, no device identifier, and nothing else you shared.

If the full link contains coordinates, the app uses them and nothing more is looked up.

Finding places

When a link has a place name or address but no coordinates, RemoteAuto sends that text to the Google Places API (places.googleapis.com) to find the place. It then asks for that place's details by the ID Google returned. The request uses our API key and names the app: its package name and the fingerprint of its signing certificate. These identify the app, not you or your phone. Requests are encrypted with https.

If the Places API finds nothing, the app asks your phone's built-in location lookup service (Android's Geocoder) with the same text. That service is part of the phone's system, not part of RemoteAuto. On most phones it is provided by Google Play services and sends the text to Google.

Google handles these requests under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or keep the text you look up.

On this device

Each place you share is saved in the app's own storage on the phone: the text exactly as it was shared, the Google Maps link, the place name and address, its coordinates, whether you marked it a favourite, how it was found, when you saved it, and when you last navigated to it.

Android backup is turned on for RemoteAuto. If you use Android's backup to your Google account, or move to a new phone with Android's transfer tool, your saved places go with it. That backup belongs to your Google account. We cannot see it.

The app writes technical messages to the phone's system log for troubleshooting. These can include the shared text, links, place names, addresses and coordinates. They stay on the phone, and RemoteAuto never sends them anywhere. Other apps cannot read them. Someone who connects the phone to a computer with developer debugging turned on can.

Waze and the car

When you tap a place on the phone, RemoteAuto opens the Waze app with that place's coordinates and asks it to start navigation. Only the coordinates are passed, not the name or the shared text. If Waze is not installed, the app tells you and opens nothing. Waze handles your trip under its own privacy policy.

In Android Auto, the car screen shows your saved places: their name and address, or the link or shared text when there is no address. When you pick one, its coordinates are handed to the navigation app you use in Android Auto. RemoteAuto sends nothing to the car maker or anyone else.

What RemoteAuto does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No server of ours. Nothing is sent to us.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, or files.

Permissions

Deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on the phone is yours to remove:

  • Press and hold a saved place, then Delete, to remove it.
  • Menu → Delete all removes every saved place.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. App info → Storage → Clear storage does the same without uninstalling.

Copies in your Android backup are managed in your Google account settings.

Children

RemoteAuto is for people 18 and over and is not directed at children.
