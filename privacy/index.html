Privacy Policy

How OpenCode Mobile — Unofficial handles data. Last updated: 11 August 2026.

This is an unofficial third-party client for opencode. It is not published by the opencode project. This page describes the mobile apps’ practices, not your opencode server.

What the apps do

OpenCode Mobile connects to an opencode server that you run or choose (for example on your development machine). Connection details you enter (host, credentials) stay on your device in platform-secured storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore-backed storage). The apps do not provide an account system or relay for that server.

Crash and diagnostics data

Release builds may send crash reports and limited automatic technical / lifecycle diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (used as Crashlytics breadcrumbs). That can include device and OS information, app version, and crash stack traces.

  • No custom product analytics events are sent today.
  • Automatic screen / page-view reporting is disabled.
  • Advertising ID / IDFA is not used for ads or ad personalization.
  • Debug builds do not include this Firebase runtime.

What we do not collect for advertising

The apps do not sell your personal data and do not use collected diagnostics for third-party advertising or ad personalization.

Your opencode server

Prompts, messages, and project content you send through the app go to the opencode server you configured. That traffic and any server- side logging are outside this privacy policy and under your (or your server operator’s) control.

Requesting deletion of diagnostics data

Release builds may send crash and limited technical diagnostics to Firebase (Crashlytics / Analytics). To request deletion of that developer-held diagnostics data associated with your use of the apps, email ivanfenenko@gmail.com with the subject line “OpenCode Mobile data deletion”, your Google Play account email if relevant, and approximate device / app version details. We will process reasonable requests for diagnostics we control. Content on your own opencode server is outside this process — delete it there.
