Install via Play Closed Testing

Get OpenCode Mobile — Unofficial through Google Play Closed testing. Anyone who joins the tester Google Group can install — this is not a public production listing.

Self-invite in two steps: join the tester Google Group, then open the Play Store listing with the same Google account. After install, follow the connection guide.

0

Before you start

What you need on the device

  • A physical Android device (or emulator) signed into Google Play
  • The Google Play Store app
  • One Google account used for both the group join and the Play Store install

Coming from an older sideloaded / Firebase APK? Uninstall that copy first. Different signing certificates won’t update in place.

1

Join the tester Google Group

This puts your account on the Play Closed testing list

Steps

  1. Open the group link while signed into the Google account you use on the Android device.
  2. Join OpenCode Mobile Testers (anyone can join).
  3. Wait a few minutes if Play still says you’re not a tester — group membership can take a short while to sync (occasionally longer).

Join OpenCode Mobile Testers

groups.google.com/g/opencode-mobile-testers
2

Install from Play Store

On your physical device

Steps

  1. Open the Play Store listing while signed into the same Google account.
  2. Accept the Closed testing invite / Become a tester if prompted.
  3. On the OpenCode Mobile — Unofficial listing, tap Install (or Update).
  4. Launch the app from Play or the app drawer.

Open on Google Play

play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.opencode.mobile
3

After install

Point the app at your opencode server

  1. Open the app and complete or skip onboarding if shown.
  2. Follow Connect to an opencode server (Tailscale recommended).
✓

Checklist

Quick confirmation

  • Joined the OpenCode Mobile Testers Google Group
  • Opened the Play Store listing with the same account
  • Old sideloaded copy uninstalled if switching
  • App installs and opens from Play
  • Server connection saved
Troubleshooting
SymptomLikely cause
Play says you’re not a tester / can’t install Not yet in the Google Group; group membership still syncing; wrong Google account signed in on the device
App listing missing or Install unavailable Not in the tester group yet; Play still syncing after join; wrong account in Play Store
Install fails / “App not installed” Existing sideloaded build still installed — uninstall it, then install from Play
Only an old build shows Newer closed-test release not rolled out yet; wait or check with the maintainer
Joined the group but Play still rejects you after an hour+ Confirm the Play Store account matches the group membership; reopen the Play listing later or ask the maintainer
