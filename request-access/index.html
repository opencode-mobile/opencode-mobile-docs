Request access

Builds are private. Fill out the form to ask for TestFlight or Firebase App Distribution access for OpenCode Mobile — Unofficial.

Access is invite-only. After you’re added, use the install guides for your platform, then connect to your opencode server.

What happens next

  1. Submit the Google Form with your details and preferred platform.
  2. Wait for an invite (App Store Connect, TestFlight, or Firebase tester email).
  3. Install with the matching guide, then point the app at your server.
