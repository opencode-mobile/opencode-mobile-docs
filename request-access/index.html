Request access

Builds are private. Choose your platform — Android self-invites via a Google Group + Play Closed testing; iOS uses a short access form for TestFlight.

Android: join the tester group yourself, then install from the Play Store listing. iOS: submit the form and wait for a TestFlight invite. Then connect to your opencode server.

What happens next

  1. Choose Android or iOS.
  2. Android: open the Play Closed guide — join the Google Group, then the Play Store listing.
  3. iOS: submit the access form, wait for a TestFlight invite, then install.
